Library concept · interactive

A map, not a filing cabinet.

Books are easy to find because the structure mirrors how people remember them: first by broad kind, then by genre, with personal collections kept as a separate layer.

01
Categories are stableEach book has one primary home such as Fiction or Non-fiction, preventing duplicates and ambiguity.
02
Genres can overlapA historical romance can appear under both Romance and Historical without making another copy.
03
Collections stay personal“Holiday reading” and “Books to revisit” are yours; they are not confused with factual metadata.
04
Grouping is only a lensReorganize All books by category, genre, series, or author without changing the books themselves.
TRY IT → Open Fiction, tap a genre, switch to All books and change “Group by”, open a cover, or search for a category.
